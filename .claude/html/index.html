Wager Schedulers

The three \Edge-Radar\ tasks that actually place bets. Times in PT (system local).

# Task Cadence Trigger What it does
1 All-Sports-SameDay-Execution daily 5:05 AM Scans NBA/NHL/MLB/NFL for today's games and places bets. --date today --budget 15% --max-bets 7 --exclude-open
2 All-Sports-NoDateFilter-Execution Mon · Thu 5:20 AM Scans all sports across all dates for multi-day edge. --budget 15% --max-bets 6 --exclude-open (no --date)
3 NextDay-Execute Sun–Thu 6:00 PM Locks in early lines for tomorrow's games. --date tomorrow --budget 10% --max-bets 6 --exclude-open

Daily fire sequence

5:05 AM  Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat  — All-Sports-SameDay-Execution      (today's games)
5:20 AM  Mon Thu                      — All-Sports-NoDateFilter-Execution  (multi-day edge)
6:00 PM  Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu          — NextDay-Execute                    (tomorrow's games)
Manual trigger commands (Git Bash) 
# Prepend MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 to stop Git Bash from translating /tn
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 schtasks /run /tn "\Edge-Radar\All-Sports-SameDay-Execution"
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 schtasks /run /tn "\Edge-Radar\All-Sports-NoDateFilter-Execution"
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 schtasks /run /tn "\Edge-Radar\NextDay-Execute"

# PowerShell equivalent (no MSYS prefix)
Start-ScheduledTask -TaskPath '\Edge-Radar\' -TaskName 'NextDay-Execute'
